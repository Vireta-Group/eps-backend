## 🏗 Current Modules

### ✅ Completed
- **Tenant Registration** — Multi-tenant registration with auto-created school admin and Sanctum token
- **Role-based Authentication** — Login, logout, user profile with role verification
- **Onboarding Wizard** — 4-step school setup (basic info, calendar, address/principal, notification/branding)
- **File Upload** — Logo, favicon, branding images stored publicly
- **API Documentation** — Auto-generated interactive docs via Scramble at `/docs/api`

### 🏗 In Progress (Module 1 — Academic Management)
- **Academic Year Management** — Create, activate, archive, auto-archive previous year, date overlap validation, structure copy tracking

### 📅 Planned
- Class, Section & Subject Management
- Student Management
- Teacher & Employee Management
- Exam & Result Management
- Fee Management
- Transport & Hostel Management
- Library, Canteen, Health & Security modules
- Analytics & Reporting
